02 / Approach

Good software is a series of things you refuse to get wrong.

The products look different because the work is different. Their behaviour comes from the same four rules.

01

Narrow beats broad.

Each product does one job for one kind of team. We prefer a focused tool that does its work well to another platform that tries to do everything.

02

The record is the product.

Exports, audit trails, and correction history are part of the design from the start, not a feature bolted on after the first request.

03

Sensitive data stays put.

We treat data location and ownership as product decisions. Local processing, clear access boundaries, and usable exports matter.

04

Plain over clever.

People should understand the screen in front of them without needing a tour. Novelty is a cost; we only spend it when it helps.

Software lab, not software theatre. Useful work over impressive demos.
