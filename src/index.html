01Products

Three products,
one standard.

None of them share a market or a codebase. All of them are live, used by real teams, and built so that the record they keep is worth something when somebody finally asks for it.

Remote Days

Cross-border remote work compliance

remotedays.app

Records where each employee actually worked, counts those days against the tax and social-security threshold of the country they live in, and warns before a limit is crossed. Built for employers in Luxembourg whose staff live in France, Belgium or Germany.

Thresholds
France and Belgium at 34 days a year, Germany at 183 — configurable per country
Evidence
An immutable log of who declared what, when, and who changed it afterwards
Access
Employee, HR and admin roles, with single sign-on through your directory

Dentra

Practice management for dental clinics

dentra.fly.dev

Patient records, appointments, treatment plans, prescriptions and GST-compliant billing in one place. Built for clinics in India that are still running the day out of a register book, a paper diary and a WhatsApp thread.

Records
History, allergy flags, X-rays and notes on a single patient profile
Billing
CGST/SGST invoices generated during the appointment, not after it
Reminders
Appointment and recall messages sent over WhatsApp and SMS automatically

Toolbit

A local-first developer toolbox

toolbit.app

Forty-two tools for JSON, JWTs, regex, encoding, conversion and inspection that run entirely inside the browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, so the config file or token you are debugging never leaves your machine.

No uploads
Input is read from disk and processed in the tab, never transmitted
Offline
Installs as an app; cached tools keep working with no connection
Licence
MIT and open source. No account, no paid tier, no advertising

02Approach

How we build.

Four rules that apply to all three products. They are why the products look unrelated and still behave the same way.

  1. 01

    Narrow beats broad

    Each product does one job for one kind of team. None of them is a platform, and none of them is a general-purpose tool with a mode for your industry.

  2. 02

    The record is the product

    Exports, audit trails and corrections are designed first, not added when a customer asks. If it cannot be handed to an inspector, it is not finished.

  3. 03

    Sensitive data stays put

    Toolbit computes in the browser. Remote Days keeps its data in the EU. Dentra’s clinics own their patient records and can export the lot whenever they want.

  4. 04

    Plain over clever

    A receptionist, an HR lead and a developer each have to understand the screen in front of them on first use. Novelty is a cost, and we mostly decline to pay it.
